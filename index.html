Hello, I'm Lai Hong Yu!



I am a student at the School of Science and Technology, Singapore, with a strong passion for software development, hardware engineering, and robotics. Additionally, I created a medicine reminder app designed to assist individuals in managing their medication schedules effectively. My goal is to leverage technology to develop innovative tools that enhance people’s lives and address real-world challenges.
